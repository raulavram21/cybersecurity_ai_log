# Hardware Attacks

## Overview

Hardware attacks use physical devices or modifications to compromise systems or steal data. Security+ expects you to recognise the common examples and what they look like.

## Malicious USB Devices

| Device | Appearance | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Malicious USB cable** | Looks and works like a normal cable | Injects code onto the computer when plugged in |
| **Malicious flash drive** | Looks like a normal flash drive | Injects code onto the computer when plugged in |

Penetration testers also build these devices to test an organization's defences.

> **Analogy:** A key that looks like a normal key but carries a hidden blade. It opens the door as expected while doing something else in the background.

## Card Cloning and Skimming

- **Card cloning:** Reading the unique identifier from an RFID badge or card and copying it onto another card
- **Skimming:** Capturing card data during a payment, using a hidden reader fitted to a card terminal

> **Analogy:** Card cloning is photocopying a key card's code. Skimming is a hidden camera recording someone typing their PIN at a cash machine.
